Local handwriting module for Label Configurator.

Application integration, selection handling, segmentation, decoding and review
are implemented in handwriting.js and handwriting-worker.js.

The pretrained recognition weights are not an original trained model of this
application. They are PP-OCRv6 Medium Cyrillic by Metrika Labs, based on
PP-OCRv6 by PaddlePaddle. See MODEL-LICENSE.txt and MODEL-NOTICE.txt.
Source: https://huggingface.co/metrika-labs/PP-OCRv6_medium_rec_cyrillic_onnx

All inference runs on the user's device. No recognition API, service account
or paid per-image endpoint is used. Training examples are stored only after
an explicit user action and are never included in the repository.
